About the model

A lumped-element electro-mechano-acoustic circuit (driver, Le, box compliance, leakage loss, port mass) is solved at every frequency. Excursion, port velocity and impedance come from the same solution as the SPL curve, so they always agree. Results are linear small-signal estimates: real drivers lose output from Bl(x) non-linearity, thermal compression and room effects. Use it to compare designs and as a starting point, then verify with measurements.
