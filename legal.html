SubForge is a design aid. Every result – frequency response, cone excursion, port velocity, box and port dimensions, cut lists, plans – is a small-signal simulation estimate based on the driver parameters you enter. Real drivers, wood, glue, room acoustics and amplifiers behave differently. Always check the result against the driver manufacturer's recommendations and, where it matters, with measurements.

Subwoofers can produce damaging sound levels and boxes can be heavy. Use hearing protection, do not exceed your driver's or amplifier's ratings, and secure heavy enclosures properly. Wiring and amplifier-load suggestions are guidance only; check them against your equipment's documentation.
